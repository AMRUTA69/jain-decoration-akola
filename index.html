Our Story

About Jain Decoration & Mandap

Founded by Nikhil Agarkar, Jain Decoration & Mandap has been crafting extraordinary celebration spaces in Akola for over a decade. What began as a passion for honouring traditional Indian ceremonies has grown into one of Akola's most trusted decoration studios.

We specialise in mandap and decoration work for weddings, engagements, religious ceremonies, and personal milestones. Every setup is crafted with meticulous attention to detail — combining traditional aesthetics with modern design sensibilities.

With over 500 events decorated and hundreds of happy families served across Akola and Vidarbha, we are honoured to be part of your most treasured moments.
